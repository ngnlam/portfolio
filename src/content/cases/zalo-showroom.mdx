<Section id="problem" title="Buyers outside the website, and their leads lost or miscounted">

Many VinFast buyers never start on the website. They chat in Zalo, work at partner banks with staff offers, or shop through the group's loyalty app. Zalo leads were being logged as Facebook, bank staff leads would have mixed in with every other web lead, and the loyalty app sent plenty of car leads that rarely turned into deposits.

</Section>

<Section id="found" title="Each channel needed its own source, and Zalo needed its own rules">

- **Zalo had no lead source of its own.** Since March 2025, leads from Zalo had been logged as Facebook.
- **Deposits change the legal rules.** Legal advised that taking online deposits brings in the full e-commerce ordering rules.
- **The Zalo build drifted from the website.** My content audit found outdated prices, missing colors and a model no longer on sale.
- **The loyalty app was invisible in reports.** It is a steady source of car leads, yet 98% of its sessions over 30 days showed up in GA4 as direct traffic.

</Section>

<Section id="decisions" title="Lead capture first, a source for every channel">

<Decisions>
<Decision title="Give legal three ways to launch">

Signing a data agreement with Zalo takes about a month. I gave legal and the data protection officer three options: go live under Zalo's public agreement and sign in parallel, drop the in-app form, or move the launch date, and recommended the first. Deposits stayed in phase 2, as the brief set from the start, since online deposits bring in the full e-commerce ordering rules.

</Decision>
<Decision title="Give every channel a lead source of its own">

Zalo leads carry the source "Zalo Mini App" plus campaign fields, and each partner bank has its own campaign rules and CRM topic, so sales and reports can tell the channels apart.

</Decision>
<Decision title="Rework the bank pages to a premium standard">

My first bank page design came back as not premium enough, so I studied offer pages from premium car brands and reworked it before launch.

</Decision>
<Decision title="Prototype the loyalty flow before building it">

A clickable prototype for cars and scooters, with a shorter form and views for each membership tier, made the real issue visible before anyone wrote code: the app team's read is that most people leave to compare prices with dealers.

</Decision>
</Decisions>

</Section>

<Section id="learned" title="The risky part was the data we reused">

- The Zalo form broke on a model list copied from an older app, not on new code. I now check reused data before new features.
- Agreeing each channel's lead source in week one meant measurement was ready before the screens were.

</Section>
