<Section id="problem" title="Customers live in Zalo. The showroom lived on the website.">

Zalo is where many Vietnamese buyers chat, but to browse or buy a VinFast they had to leave it for the website. VinFast approved a sales channel inside Zalo in August, and I became its business owner in September. My bar: every lead lands in the same sales system as every other channel, counted as Zalo.

</Section>

<Section id="found" title="Zalo leads were hiding under Facebook">

- **Zalo had no lead source of its own.** More than 1,000 leads since March 2025 came from Zalo but were logged as Facebook.
- **Deposits change the legal rules.** Legal advised that taking online deposits brings in the full e-commerce ordering rules.
- **The build drifted from the website.** My content audit found outdated prices, missing colors and a model no longer on sale.

</Section>

<Section id="decisions" title="Lead capture first, deposits later">

<Decisions>
<Decision title="Split the launch into two phases">

Phase 1 is a home screen, a listing, a page for each model and a form. I moved deposits to phase 2 because a new sales system was due in October, and a deposit flow built before it would have been built twice.

</Decision>
<Decision title="Keep the form inside the app">

Linking out to the website form would have opened a web page inside the Zalo app, which is slower and loses the Zalo context. The form lives in the app.

</Decision>
<Decision title="Give Zalo a lead source of its own">

Every lead carries the source "Zalo Mini App" plus campaign fields, so reports can separate Zalo from Facebook without guessing.

</Decision>
<Decision title="Collect only the Zalo user ID">

The form sends data straight to VinFast's servers and asks Zalo for nothing beyond the user ID, so no data processing agreement with Zalo was needed.

</Decision>
</Decisions>

</Section>

<Section id="results" title="Signed off, with a launch plan approved">

<UatTables />

- All 95 checks passed in late October. Testing caught the form accepting only two models, because it reused a model list from an older app; mapping all 81 product codes fixed it before sign-off.
- Go-live moved to November for the build team's cloud setup. My launch plan was approved on 18 November: the Zalo Official Account menu, its chatbot, a broadcast to followers and inbox ads all point buyers to the app.
- **Next:** lead volume from launch day, counted through Zalo's own lead source.

<Section id="learned" title="The risky part was the data we reused">

- The form broke on a model list copied from an older app, not on new code. I now check reused data before new features.
- Agreeing the lead source in week one meant measurement was ready before the screens were.

</Section>
