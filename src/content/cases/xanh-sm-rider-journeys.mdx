<Section id="problem" title="A marketplace that needed drivers and riders at the same time">

Xanh SM is Vietnam's electric ride-hailing app. To grow, it needed more drivers to meet demand and more riders who keep riding. Driver hiring leaned on offline channels, and rider messaging ran on a calendar: the same push to everyone on the same day, judged by click-through rate.

</Section>

<Section id="found" title="Drivers were online already, and riders differ by what they last did">

- **Drivers scroll the same feeds riders do.** Online channels brought only about a quarter of new Bike drivers, so the cheapest channel was the least used.
- **Never-ridden riders differ by intent.** Some never opened the app after signing up; some checked prices; some requested a ride that did not happen. Each needs a different nudge.
- **Lapsed riders differ by habit.** A frequent rider gone 21 days is a different case from an occasional rider gone 28.
- **Food orders stalled for lack of a reason to continue, not for bugs.** Across 211,272 add-to-cart events, the drop-off pointed to missing prompts such as free delivery and to unfamiliar restaurants.

<Section id="decisions" title="Journeys built on behavior, judged on trips">

<Decisions>
<Decision title="Recruit drivers where they already are">

I led the seeding content as Bike driver recruitment shifted online: 145 pieces in one month, placed 7,250 times in the community groups drivers read.

</Decision>
<Decision title="Enter journeys by behavior, not by calendar">

Each rider enters the journey that matches their last action. Riders who never opened the app get no journey, because there is nothing to respond to.

</Decision>
<Decision title="Create a first trip, don't borrow one">

I turned down booking a first trip on a new rider's behalf, because it only moves demand from one person to another instead of creating it.

</Decision>
<Decision title="Judge every journey on trips">

Every week I tracked opens, clicks and trips completed for each journey, judged it on trips, and raised or lowered its sending frequency based on that.

</Decision>
<Decision title="Fixed send times, approved offers, no guilt">

Messages go out at 8 am, 11 am or 7 pm. Offers come only from the approved matrix for each of four market groups. Lapsed riders never hear "we miss you"; they hear what the deal is.

</Decision>
</Decisions>

</Section>

<Section id="shipped" title="30+ journeys, a driver content engine and a food cart plan">

- **Journeys in CleverTap:** 14 for never-ridden and lapsed riders by September 2025, plus three for the team in the Philippines, and more than 30 across rider groups, provinces and services (bike, car, express and food) by April 2026.
- **Food cart:** a four-fix plan for the cart, led by a free-delivery prompt right after add to cart and timed cart reminders.
- **Driver content:** 145 pieces and 7,250 placements in driver groups for Bike drivers in a single month.
- **Tone rules and a prompt template** so AI tools draft push and Zalo notification messages in the team's voice.

<Section id="learned" title="Grouping riders by their last action showed which journey each one needed">

- Splitting riders by their last action turned scattered journeys into one system, mapped by province and service.
- Writing the tone rules down, with a prompt template, let AI tools draft in the team's voice without me rewriting every message.

</Section>
