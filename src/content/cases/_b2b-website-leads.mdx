<Section id="problem" title="No tracking, and enquiries buried in an inbox">

For a company selling robot training data, the website is where an AI lab first asks for a sample. In April it had no event tracking, and enquiries reached BD late, mixed with job applications.

</Section>

<Section id="found" title="Buyers read first, then ask for a sample">

- **Buyers research before they talk.** Put the right offer, a free sample, where they read.
- **"Saw the form" couldn't be measured.** The only form sat at the bottom of every page.
- **Leads arrived without context.** No field said what someone wanted, and job seekers used the same form.

</Section>

<Section id="decisions" title="A free sample, offered in four places">

<Decisions>
<Decision title="A sample, not a newsletter">

Someone asking for data is telling you what they want to buy.

</Decision>
<Decision title="Four places, ranked">

P1: two menu buttons and a form on each dataset page. P2: an exit popup. P3: a chat bubble. Encord and Labelbox use the same menu pattern.

</Decision>
<Decision title="Fewer fields where intent is lower">

Two on the popup, four on the sample form, five on Talk to our team.

</Decision>
<Decision title="Count AI citations, not clicks">

68% of 266 target keywords had no search volume: AI labs ask assistants, not Google. I made how often AI assistants cite VinDynamics the search goal.

</Decision>
<Decision title="Track intent, nothing else">

13 events across discovery, evaluation and lead capture. I cut video plays, scrolls and page resizes.

</Decision>
</Decisions>

- The first B2B meeting request through the site came in mid-June and moved to an NDA the next week.
- A speed audit I ran traced slow mobile pages to reCAPTCHA loading three times; after the vendor's fixes, the mobile score went from 27 to 49.
- **Not proven yet:** a rise in enquiry volume. 96% of people who opened the form never started it, so the first fields are the next fix.

</Section>

<Section id="learned" title="Fix the measurement before judging the redesign">

- Test traffic and job seekers made up a big share of early "leads". Any before and after built on them would have been noise.
- Tracking and speed belong in the first brief. Now I write the tracking spec alongside the sitemap.

</Section>
